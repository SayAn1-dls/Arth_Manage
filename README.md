# Arth Manage

**Arth Manage** is a hackathon management software, an operating system for running hackathons, built for colleges and universities.

It gives organizers one place to plan, run and wrap up campus hackathons, and gives participants, mentors and judges a simple way to take part.

## Features

- **Event setup**: create hackathons with themes, tracks, timelines and rules
- **Registration**: individual and team sign-ups, with approval and check-in
- **Team management**: form teams, invite members, track participation
- **Project submissions**: submit repos, demos and presentations before the deadline
- **Judging**: scoring rubrics, judge assignment and automatic leaderboards
- **Mentors and volunteers**: assign mentors to teams and manage volunteer roles
- **Announcements**: share schedules and updates with everyone
- **Results and certificates**: publish winners and issue participation certificates
- **Analytics**: participation, submission and outcome reports for the institution

## Who It's For

| Role | What they do |
|------|--------------|
| Organizers / Admins | Create and manage events, review registrations, publish results |
| Participants | Register, form teams, submit projects |
| Mentors | Guide teams during the event |
| Judges | Evaluate submissions and score projects |
